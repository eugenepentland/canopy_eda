Physical heatsink
The thermal pad couples every PCB thermal cell beneath the drawn rectangle to the first block. “Two solid blocks” centers the lower rectangle beneath it for a board-to-case cold plate. The thermal estimate treats exposed metal as convection area; actual enclosure cooling still depends on the case temperature and case-to-air path.
Axial cooling fan
The rectangle is the fan outlet projected onto the target; the circle positions it in board coordinates. Distance is measured from the outlet face to the PCB normally, or to the sink's outer face when the fan and a board-mounted heatsink share the selected face. Installed-flow fraction accounts for enclosure and flow restriction until a measured system operating point is available.
